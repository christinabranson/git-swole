THE IDEA
A training log, not a dashboard. Hairlines instead of cards, one accent, and numbers big enough to read from arm's length with a bar in your hands.
WHAT'S DIFFERENT
The session screen is one set at a time. Weight, plate math and a loaded-bar diagram sit at the top, a thumb-sized stepper holds the reps, and one button logs it. No aiming at rows.
Under it, the whole session is one row per exercise with warm-up and working sets as cells. Logging walks the queue in order, across exercises, and any cell can be tapped out of order.
Rest owns the screen and scales to the set you just logged: all reps gets a short green ring, missed reps a long amber one. Warm-ups are computed off today's weight and never count toward volume or progression.
Reps are per-set cells, green when you hit the target and amber when you didn't, so a bad day is visible at a glance.
Program items are tagged [BB] [BW] [TIME] [DIST]; 5×5 progression applies only to [BB].
